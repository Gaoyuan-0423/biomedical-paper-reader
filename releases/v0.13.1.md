# v0.13.1

## 中文

修复推理模型读图与长文整理的生成中断问题。相对 v0.13.0，这次更新保留已有文库、笔记、模型设置和外观功能。

- 对智谱官方标准接口中的 `glm-5.3`／`glm-5.3-flash`，读图预算调整为 8192 tokens；仅在长度耗尽且没有正式答案时重试一次，重试上限 16384。其他端点和模型保留原首轮预算。
- 同一适配范围内，长文每段整理使用 16384 tokens，长度耗尽时从相同原文重试一次，上限 32768。中间整理使用轻量推理，最终精读报告、读图和标签的推理参数不变。其他服务及模型不自动接收额外推理参数。
- 非流式回答检查结束状态，区分截断、空答案、拒绝回答、服务错误和格式异常；不把推理过程或截断正文保存为完整笔记。
- 分段整理失败或取消后停止其他未完成任务，避免继续发送排队请求。开始整理时立即显示阶段提示；耗尽重试后明确报告整理阶段的限制，保留原笔记。
- 保留原文页码、来源编号过滤、最终证据绑定以及并发修改保护。

## English

Fixes generation interruptions during figure analysis and long-document condensation with reasoning models. This maintenance update from v0.13.0 preserves existing libraries, notes, model settings and appearance preferences.

- For the exact `glm-5.3` and `glm-5.3-flash` models on the official standard BigModel endpoint, figure analysis receives an 8192-token budget. A confirmed length limit with no final answer permits one retry capped at 16384. Other endpoints and models retain their initial budget.
- Within the same supported scope, each condensation chunk receives 16384 tokens and may be regenerated once from the same source at a 32768-token limit. Intermediate condensation uses light reasoning; final reports, figure analysis and tags retain their existing reasoning parameters. Other services and models do not receive additional reasoning parameters automatically.
- Non-streaming replies are checked for completion. Truncation, empty answers, refusals, service errors and incompatible response formats are reported separately. Reasoning text and truncated answers are not saved as complete notes.
- Failed or cancelled condensation runs cancel outstanding tasks and stop queued requests. A stage message appears when condensation starts, and an exhausted retry reports the affected stage while preserving existing notes.
- Original page references, source-ID filtering, final evidence binding and concurrent-edit protection remain in place.
